# Sources — Who Should Draw Your Voting District?

_Every claim that carries a number or a name should trace to a source here. Group by claim._
_Each was opened and saved during the 2026-10-19 peg hunt and hand passes (A: the count and each state's
status; B: the veto settings, the peg, the evidence on commissions, polling), or during the 2026-10-20 top-up
hand pass C (the 2025–26 sequence, Utah's Proposition 4, seats vs votes), or during the 2026-10-20 top-up hand
pass D (court findings on congressional maps, both directions); the fact-check log gives the exact quote and the
verdict._

> Verification note: each source is marked [primary] (the original data/report) or [secondary]
> (reporting on it). Primary is preferred for anything quoted on screen. Several official pages refused
> automated fetches and were read through a text-rendering proxy or with browser headers; the text was
> saved and quoted from that copy. Where a page's address could not be recovered from the saved copy, the
> entry names the publisher and title and says "saved copy". Dossier claims that neither hand pass
> adjudicated are not listed here, because they are not on air.
