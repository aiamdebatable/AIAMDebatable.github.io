AI and Artists: Which Claims Hold Up?

Which of the claims people repeat about AI and creative jobs hold up?

Last changed 2026-11-04 · See every change

▶ Rewatch on YouTube

Eight claims people repeat about AI and the people who make things, from Getty's bankruptcy to Miyazaki's 'insult to life', each stamped true, partly true, not measured or false.

The verdict

Where the record leans
Where the record leans: the flood of AI work is real where it's been counted. The cheap, short, commissioned jobs are the ones going. The courts haven't decided. And no tool keeps your work safe.

In the judge's own words. The whole verdict, as it aired, is below.

▶ Rewatch the verdict at 13:13 on YouTube
Read the verdict as it aired

Erin So, Raj. Eight claims. Which ones can I stop repeating?

Raj Two. AI didn't bankrupt Getty, by Getty's own account. And the game actors' strike never stopped being about AI.

Erin And which ones hold up?

Raj Miyazaki's line, with its date and its demo. And freelance writing work really did fall, after ChatGPT arrived.

Erin And everything in between?

Raj Glaze, the placeholders, Pinterest and Spotify each have a real core, and a piece people add. And whether AI is wiping out creative jobs, nobody has measured yet.

Raj Where the record leans: the flood of AI work is real where it's been counted. The cheap, short, commissioned jobs are the ones going. The courts haven't decided. And no tool keeps your work safe.

Erin And the next time one of these lands in my feed?

Raj Ask three things before you share it. Whose account is this? Is it a forecast, or a count of what happened? And did anyone actually tie it to AI?

Erin And the joke? AI doing the art while I do the dishes?

Raj The worry under it is fair. For some freelance writers, it's already true. For most of the rest, it's still an open question. Check which one you're sharing.

The judge named no sides on this one, so there's no dial: we don't invent positions.
