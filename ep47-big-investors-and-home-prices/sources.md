> Verification note: mark each source as [primary] (the original data/report) or [secondary]
> (reporting on it). Prefer primary for anything quoted on-screen.
> Three further marks sit beside those: **[vendor]** a data company selling housing data; **[advocacy]**
> an organisation that campaigns on the question, or a bill's sponsor speaking about the bill;
> **[trade]** a trade association or trade press. Several government sites refused automated fetching:
> GAO's reports and senate.gov's roll-call files were read in web-archive copies of the publishers' own

> is marked **(locator)**, the hand pass recorded the document but not this exact address: it is the
> publisher's page or file for that document, and should be re-confirmed before the research bundle
