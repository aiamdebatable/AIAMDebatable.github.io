Sources — Housing Market: Will Banning Big Investors Make Homes Cheaper?

Every claim that carries a number or a name should trace to a source here. Group by claim. "Log" numbers refer to the rows of factcheck-log.md. Every legal status is as of 2026-10-26.

Verification note: mark each source as [primary] (the original data/report) or [secondary] (reporting on it). Prefer primary for anything quoted on-screen. Three further marks sit beside those: [vendor] a data company selling housing data; [advocacy] an organisation that campaigns on the question, or a bill's sponsor speaking about the bill; [trade] a trade association or trade press. Several government sites refused automated fetching: GAO's reports and senate.gov's roll-call files were read in web-archive copies of the publishers' own files, and SEC filings and several web pages through a text-reader proxy (the publisher's own text). Every document below was saved, with its text extracted, in the show's evidence archive. Where a link is marked (locator), the hand pass recorded the document but not this exact address: it is the publisher's page or file for that document, and should be re-confirmed before the research bundle publishes.
