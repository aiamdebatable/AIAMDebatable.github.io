MaraStart with the island, because most people have never heard of it. Isle Royale is a rock forty-five miles long in the middle of Lake Superior, Michigan's, but closer to Canada. MaraCompletely. November to April, the only national park in the country that shuts for the winter. And by law it's wilderness, almost the whole island, since nineteen seventy-six: a place, in the words of the act, where man is a visitor who does not remain.

NiaSo why is the science here?

MaraBecause it's a closed system. No bears, no deer, no hunting, no roads. One predator, one large prey.
