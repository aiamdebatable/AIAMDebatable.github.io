The verdict

Where the record leans
Where the record leans: the hit lands first on the short, cheap, high-volume jobs, and AI-made work is growing far faster than what it pays. Whether AI destroys more creative work than it creates, the record can't say.
What didn't hold up
That voice acting is dying: nobody counts voice work. That AI songs are draining royalties everywhere: the case was bots, and Deezer says the hit to human royalties there is minimal. That the label deals pay musicians: the players are suing. That AI creates creators: nobody has measured it.

Read the verdict as it aired

Cole No winner today. First what's settled, then what held up on each side, and then a dial that belongs to you.

Cole Settled: at the cheap end, a machine now costs a sliver of a person's rate. The flood of AI-made work is real, and most of it earns little. The cleanest count, salaried designers, fell about one in thirteen in a year. And nobody has counted the work that exists only because of AI.

Cole What held up for green. The freelance studies found writing work down, sharpest for short jobs. Revenue per selling ebook fell even for books with no AI. And the new payouts are thin: zero was the most common answer for what a synthetic voice earned.

Cole What held up for gold. The broad data shows no collapse. Game layoffs came with other reasons, and one image study found no effect. The tools lift beginners most. And people still rate work higher when they're told a person made it.

Cole What didn't hold up. That voice acting is dying: nobody counts voice work. That AI songs are draining royalties everywhere: the case was bots, and Deezer says the hit to human royalties there is minimal. That the label deals pay musicians: the players are suing. That AI creates creators: nobody has measured it.

Cole Where the record leans: the hit lands first on the short, cheap, high-volume jobs, and AI-made work is growing far faster than what it pays. Whether AI destroys more creative work than it creates, the record can't say.

Cole So the dial: when you watch, listen, read or hire, how much does it matter to you that a person made it? Not at all, just make it: cheap, but the flood mostly earns nothing. Tell me when it's AI: Spotify labels AI artists, though it admits its detection has limits.

Cole Pay the people it learned from: union terms and the Anthropic settlement, though payouts so far are thin. Only if a person made it: Deezer is building that switch, yet nearly everyone in its blind test couldn't tell. Where buyers set this dial decides who gets paid. Where you set yours is your call.

The judge named no sides on this one, so there's no dial: we don't invent positions.
