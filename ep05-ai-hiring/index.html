The claims
  • Confirmed

    ~51% of companies use AI in hiring (2024); ~68% projected by end of 2025; survey of ~1,000 leaders

  • Confirmed

    ~76% AI-written interview questions; ~69% AI candidate assessments (among adopters)

  • Confirmed

    ~67% of AI-hiring adopters admit their tools could introduce bias

  • Confirmed

    Amazon scrapped a recruiting AI (2018) that penalized "women's"; trained on ~10 yrs mostly-male resumes

  • Confirmed

    iTutorGroup EEOC settlement: $365,000; 200+ applicants auto-rejected by age; first EEOC AI-hiring settlement (2023)

  • Confirmed

    Mobley v. Workday (2025): federal court let a nationwide ADEA case proceed as a collective action; tool can act as employer's "agent"

  • Confirmed

    University of Washington audit (2024): 3M+ comparisons; 3 commercial LLMs; white names preferred 85% vs 9% Black; male 52% vs 11% female; Black-male names never favored over white-male

  • Confirmed

    GPT-4 ranked disability-related resumes lower; bias reduced via fairness fine-tuning (FAccT 2024)

  • Confirmed

    Bertrand & Mullainathan: white-sounding names got 50% more callbacks; a white name ≈ 8 extra years of experience

  • Confirmed

    Disparate impact: Title VII liability without intent; four-fifths (80%) rule is a "rule of thumb," not dispositive

  • Confirmed

    NYC Local Law 144 requires a published bias audit + candidate notice, but sets NO minimum ratio and certifies nothing

  • Confirmed

    Under 5% of NYC employers published an audit (18 of 391 audits, 13 notices)

  • Confirmed

    A US agency (EEOC) removed its AI-hiring technical-assistance guidance (Jan 2025)

  • Confirmed

    The EU pushed its high-risk employment-AI obligations back (to Dec 2027)

  • Unresolved

    Mobley collective opt-in count (~14,000)

  • Unresolved

    "Billion+ rejected applications" scale figure
