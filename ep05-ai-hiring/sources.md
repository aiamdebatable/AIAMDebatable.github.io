# Sources — "AI in hiring: ban it, or bind it?"

_Grouped by claim. Every on-screen number and named case traces here. Verdicts in `factcheck-log.md`._

## Adoption / landscape
- ~51% use AI in hiring (2024), ~68% projected end-2025; ~76% interview questions, ~69% assessments;
  ~67% admit possible bias (survey of ~1,000 leaders) — ResumeBuilder:
  https://www.resumebuilder.com/7-in-10-companies-will-use-ai-in-the-hiring-process-in-2025-despite-most-saying-its-biased/
- HireVue dropped facial-expression analysis (2021); nonverbal cues ≈0.25% of predictive power (context) — SHRM:
  https://www.shrm.org/topics-tools/news/talent-acquisition/hirevue-discontinues-facial-analysis-screening

## The named cases
- Amazon scrapped a recruiting AI that downgraded "women's" (2018); trained on ~10 yrs mostly-male
  resumes — Reuters: https://www.reuters.com/article/us-amazon-com-jobs-automation-insight-idUSKCN1MK08G ;
  mirror: https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/37/
- iTutorGroup EEOC settlement $365,000; 200+ applicants; first EEOC AI-hiring settlement (2023) — EEOC:
  https://www.eeoc.gov/newsroom/itutorgroup-pay-365000-settle-eeoc-discriminatory-hiring-suit ;
  Greenberg Traurig: https://www.gtlaw.com/en/insights/2023/8/eeoc-secures-first-workplace-artificial-intelligence-settlement
- Mobley v. Workday: nationwide ADEA collective certified (May 2025, Judge Rita Lin, N.D. Cal.); tool
  as employer "agent" — SHRM: https://www.shrm.org/topics-tools/news/technology/workday-ai-lawsuit-wake-up-call-hr ;
  Miami Law Review: https://lawreview.law.miami.edu/help-wanted-screened-by-algorithms-mobley-v-workday-and-the-legal-limits-of-ai-hiring/

## The bias evidence
- University of Washington audit (Wilson & Caliskan, AIES 2024): 3M+ comparisons; white names 85% vs
  9% Black; male 52% vs 11% female; Black-male never favored over white-male — UW:
  https://www.washington.edu/news/2024/10/31/ai-bias-resume-screening-race-gender/
- GPT-4 disability-resume penalty; reducible via fairness fine-tuning (FAccT 2024, Glazko et al.) — UW CREATE:
  https://create.uw.edu/create-researchers-find-chatgpt-biased-against-resumes-that-imply-disability-models-improvement/
- Bertrand & Mullainathan: white-sounding names 50% more callbacks; ~5,000 resumes; white name ≈ 8 extra
  years of experience — AEA: https://www.aeaweb.org/articles?id=10.1257%2F0002828042002561

## The two loaded concepts
- Disparate impact (no intent required); four-fifths (80%) rule is a "rule of thumb" — Mayer Brown:
  https://www.mayerbrown.com/en/insights/publications/2023/07/eeoc-issues-title-vii-guidance-on-employer-use-of-ai-other-algorithmic-decisionmaking-tools
- NYC Local Law 144: bias audit + notice, no minimum ratio, certifies nothing; under 5% compliance
  (18 audits, 13 notices of 391 employers) — arXiv (Null Compliance):
  https://arxiv.org/pdf/2406.01399 ; NY State Comptroller (enforcement "ineffective"):
  https://www.osc.ny.gov/state-agencies/audits/2025/12/02/enforcement-local-law-144-automated-employment-decision-tools

## Moving regulatory picture (stated as current, provisional)
- EEOC removed its AI technical-assistance guidance (~Jan 2025); underlying laws still apply — Nat'l Law Review:
  https://natlawreview.com/article/federal-government-quietly-removed-its-ai-hiring-guidance-four-states-are-writing
- EU AI Act: employment AI is high-risk (Annex III.4); high-risk obligations deferred to Dec 2027 —
  https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/annex/3/ ;
  https://www.morganlewis.com/pubs/2026/06/changes-to-eu-ai-act-deadlines-what-it-means-for-employers-and-hr-technology-providers
