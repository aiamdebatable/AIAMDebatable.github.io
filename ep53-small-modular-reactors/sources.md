**How to read this list.** Each entry is publisher — title — date — URL — [source type] — the fact-check
rows that rely on it ("A16" is hand pass A, row 16). Every source below was opened by a hand pass and
saved, with its text extracted, to the show's evidence archive (kept privately, not part of the published
bundle); every quote the rows rely on was machine-checked against that saved copy (567/567 for the five
hand passes; 39/39, 76/76 and 79/79 for the three top-ups, T1–T3). Where a
publisher's site refused automated reading, the entry says how the document was read (a plain-text relay
of an SEC filing, an archived copy, a newswire distribution copy, or tool-relayed excerpts). "Saved copy
(URL not recorded by the hand pass)" means the document was read and saved but its address was not logged;
it is cited by publisher, title and date. NRC documents are cited by their ADAMS accession number; the
links follow the NRC's standard document address for that number. A source that lists rows from several
passes is shown once, under the first.

> Verification note: **[primary]** = the original document, data or ruling; **[secondary]** = reporting
> on it; advocacy groups, industry bodies, interested companies and think tanks are labelled as such, on
> both sides. Prefer primary for anything quoted on screen. **Tool-relayed excerpts** (X-energy's
> prospectus, Oklo's 10-Q, the Army's Janus article, East Idaho News on Project Pele) are
> secondary-strength: SEC EDGAR refused requests without an e-mail contact, and army.mil and East Idaho
> News refused direct reads. One NRC document is **withheld**: see the end of this list.
