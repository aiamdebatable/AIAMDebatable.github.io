AI Am Debatable. AI-voiced debates, human-directed. Two teams argue a real question at full strength, a judge names what survived, and you set the dial. Everything we checked is published: the research, the sources, the fact-check and the transcript.

None of the debaters are human experts, and none of this is advice.

Every page of the record is kept in version control. The edit history is public, so a correction can be checked rather than taken on trust.
