⛔ CORRECTION 2026-11-08, after publication: "There is no separate Codex setting. One toggle covers both."

Found when we compared our episodes. The hand pass read OpenAI's Codex help page, which says "Your ChatGPT training data controls apply to content processed through Codex", and found no Codex-only setting there. OpenAI's data-use page, "How your data is used to improve model performance" (https://help.openai.com/en/articles/5722486-how-your-data-is-used-to-improve-model-performance), updated about 2026-09-21, before this episode aired, says (Wayback copy of 2026-09-24, the air day, and live on 2026-11-08): "Codex has a separate Include environments setting in Codex settings for allowing training on full environments. Changing your settings in ChatGPT or the Privacy Portal does not change that setting." The same page says the ChatGPT controls are "sufficient for ChatGPT conversations and Codex tasks". So the first half of the aired line holds: the ChatGPT switch covers Codex conversations and tasks. The second half does not: training on full environments has its own Codex setting, and turning off the ChatGPT switch does not change it. The page does not say where that setting starts. The published video carries the line; corrected here, in the transcript note and on the website.

claim (as aired)verdict
"There is no separate Codex setting. One toggle covers both."CORRECTED (after publication). The ChatGPT training switch covers Codex conversations and tasks, but Codex has a separate "Include environments" setting for training on full environments, and changing the ChatGPT switch does not change it. https://help.openai.com/en/articles/5722486-how-your-data-is-used-to-improve-model-performance
