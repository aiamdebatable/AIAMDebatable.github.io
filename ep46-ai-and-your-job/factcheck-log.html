Fairness audit

  • The record is lopsided, and the brief says so. On "is AI causing mass unemployment now?" every aggregate series and every null study points one way, and so do the alarm side's own best sources (Amodei, Goldman, the Canaries authors). The brief does not manufacture a 50/50 there. It places the genuine split where the evidence puts it: whether the entry-level gap is AI, and how fast change comes.
  • The engine's imbalance was fixed before the steelman. The engine argued the alarm side mostly from relays (Amodei via a Yahoo copy of Axios, Goldman via Fortune).
