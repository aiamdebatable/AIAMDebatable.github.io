The verdict

RajThis one doesn't resolve, and neither team wins it. Three things from me: what's settled, what survived, and what's yours.

The field's survey, which fails on who answers it. Or the forecasters, who can't yet show they're any good at this either.

RajSo the question we're handing you isn't whether it's ten percent. It's whose number you start from, and what the next alarm has to show you before you move it. That one's yours.
