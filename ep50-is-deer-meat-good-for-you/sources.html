Verification note: each source is marked [primary] (the original data, study, rule, release or report), [secondary] (reporting on it, or a reproduction), [industry or hunting group] (a trade body, an industry marketer or a hunting organisation, including hunting media) or [advocacy]. Prefer primary for anything quoted on screen. Every primary relied on was downloaded and saved with its text extracted, in the show's evidence archive (not part of the published bundle); "saved copy" names that file. Where the saved copy did not record the page's own address, the entry says "link not recorded in the saved copy" and gives the publisher, title and identifier instead: find it by title, and never cite a guessed link. Several government sites refused automated fetching (CDC, APHIS, foodsafety.gov, FRED, michigan.gov in places); those pages were read through a text-reader proxy (r.jina.ai), which returns the site's own text. Every USDA FoodData Central value comes from FDC's own data endpoint, not a mirror site. The engine's machine dossier (deep-research-v3, run wf_2a6f7add-971) is not a source: it was read as a list of claims to test.
