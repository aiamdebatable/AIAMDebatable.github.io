- **Neither side is written beyond its evidence.** The harm side's strongest figures come from adult
  trials and one college quasi-experiment, and the brief says so every time; its advocacy-framed sources
  (the World Happiness Report chapter that calls itself "the case for the prosecution", the After Babel
