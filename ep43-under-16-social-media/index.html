The verdict

What the record supports
This is precaution or proof: how much risk justifies acting before the evidence is in, and what you accept in return. And a limit lasts only as long as whoever holds its key wants it to.
What didn't hold up
Social media isn't proven to cause the teen crisis, and it isn't harmless. Australia's law hasn't been shown to work or to backfire. Florida didn't ban under-sixteens, chatbots aren't proven to push kids over the edge, and a two-hour default isn't a hard cap.

The contact harms are reported at scale, chatbot harm is alleged, and nothing yet measures an age line reducing any of them. Teen sadness peaked in twenty twenty-one and has fallen since.

Kate What survived from green. These harms aren't mood averages: most sextortion victims had no known mental-health condition. The trials and the teen data point one way, toward real harm. And opt-in tools reach almost nobody, so a parent can't hold a line alone.

Kate What survived from gold. Nothing yet shows an age line clearing the worst smoke, and the victims' ages run past sixteen. Every setting leaks without a reliable age check, the check is weakest exactly at the line it's drawing, and its fix reaches adults.

Kate What fell. Social media isn't proven to cause the teen crisis, and it isn't harmless. Australia's law hasn't been shown to work or to backfire. Florida didn't ban under-sixteens, chatbots aren't proven to push kids over the edge, and a two-hour default isn't a hard cap.

Kate So here's what the record supports. This is precaution or proof: how much risk justifies acting before the evidence is in, and what you accept in return. And a limit lasts only as long as whoever holds its key wants it to.

Kate A single dial is left: until what age, and who holds the key. The law, off until sixteen, as in Australia. A parent, through a line with a consent key, a guardian account, or a parent-held default. Or the teen, where a limit is a passcode away.

Kate No source in the record picks sixteen, and none earns a right answer. Precaution says act now and fix the leaks; proof says show it reaches the harm first. Until what age, and who holds the key, is a dial only you can set.
