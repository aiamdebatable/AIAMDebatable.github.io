AI Data Center Backlash: Homegrown, or Fed From Abroad?

The question: X says it removed a 200,000-account suspected-Chinese bot farm, 200 of them pushing data-center grid-and-price claims; OpenAI found the same operation and no evidence it reached anyone. Same record, two readings: a foreign hand on American anger, or a non-story dressed as a national threat. Which does the record support?

▶ Watch on YouTubeThe full record ↓

X said 200,000 Chinese bots. 200 posted about data centers. Nobody read them. Then Congress wrote to the Sierra Club.

Why anyone is asking now

When this episode came out in September 2026, X had just said it removed a bot farm of about 200,000 suspected Chinese accounts, 200 of them posting about data centers. Within a week the claim reached the President's feed, and a House committee asked the Sierra Club for its donor list.

The fight

Green arguesA non-story

X's 200 data-center accounts were the same operation OpenAI found in June: a few hundred posts in December and January, at least 59 accounts with zero engagement. Seven in ten Americans, a majority in every party, opposed one nearby before anyone said China. And the foreign money is European climate philanthropy with no data center in any grant.

Gold arguesA foreign hand

OpenAI's report shows a contractor for Chinese provincial governments built accounts to push household-bill and grid-strain messages, and wrote a plan to expand onto Facebook. The same kind of actor tried twice to rally Americans against a Texas plant, and failed. The finding of no reach comes from interested parties, and nobody searched Facebook, where the local fights live.

What both sides quietly agree on

A China-linked operation existed: a few hundred posts, most read by no one, suspended since, and disclosed twice. The foreign money named is European climate philanthropy, and the government referee that would grade foreign influence is gone. Seven in ten Americans, a majority in every party, opposed a data center nearby before anyone said China.

What the shouting gets wrong

The record supports this much: China-linked accounts tried, and the effort was tiny. By the professionals' own scale it never left its own accounts, while the reaction to it reached a cabinet secretary, two House committees and the President. What's left is how much reach a foreign operation must show before it's allowed to explain your neighbours' anger.

The verdict

Watched it already? Read the verdict as it aired

Mara This one doesn't resolve, and neither team wins it. Three things from me: what's settled, what survived, and what's yours.

Mara Both teams accepted the same record. A China-linked operation existed, and its discoverers say so. It was a few hundred posts, most read by no one, last winter, suspended since. One operation, disclosed twice. The foreign money is European climate philanthropy, with no data center in any grant. The referee that would grade this is gone. And seven in ten Americans, a majority in every party, opposed one nearby before anyone said China.

Mara What survived from green. Cause comes before effect, and this accusation came after every poll and every governor. The reach in this story belongs to the reaction, not the operation. And the money and the bots are two accusations that don't touch.

Mara What survived from gold. Intent is on paper: a written plan for Facebook, and a precedent that tried twice. No evidence of breakout is a null finding by interested parties, and nobody searched where reach would show. And a request for documents is not a verdict.

Mara What fell. Green's certainty: he can't show that nobody read them, and he said so. Gold's weight: she asked you to weigh a category one as more than one, and she said so.

Mara So here's what the record supports. It happened, and it was tiny. What's contested is what a real but tiny operation licenses: a document demand on the Sierra Club, a presidential explanation for a majority, or nothing.

Mara That leaves one dial neither team can set for you. How much reach must a foreign operation show before its existence is allowed to explain your neighbours? One post? One platform? One real reader? One changed vote?

Mara So the question we're handing you isn't bots or neighbours. Both happened. It's this: when someone tells you the anger at the county meeting isn't really yours, what would they have to show you before you believed them? That one's yours.
