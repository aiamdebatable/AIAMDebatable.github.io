ep18 — Is the data-center backlash homegrown — or fed from abroad?

Why now

The foreign-hand claim moved from platforms to Congress and the President in one week, and it now rests on documents a viewer can read.

  • ★ House Science Committee letters, 10 Sep 2026 (Chairman Babin, Subcommittee Chairman McCormick) to the NSF, Indivisible Project, Sierra Club, 350.org and Oil Change International: documents on "foreign principals", donors and FARA analyses; a briefing from NSF on foreign-affiliated NGOs under CHIPS and Science Act authorities. The release cites X Corp's 28 Aug disclosure, OpenAI's June report, the American Energy Institute's "$39M+ from foreign donors" and Americans for Public Trust's "$2bn from five foreign charities". No deadlines in the release; the letters themselves were not located. https://science.house.gov/2026/9/babin-mccormick-probe-foreign-influence-in-u-s-ai-and-energy-policy
  • ★ The President, 31 Aug (Truth Social, 09:59 EDT): "China could not be happier with this anti Data Center movement. Actually, they can't believe it is happening!" — and ○ on Fox/Ingraham, c. 10–11 Sep: "a lot of people say it's China … a China PR thing." https://www.trumpstruth.org/statuses/41411
  • ★ X Global Government Affairs, 27 Aug 2026, 7:17 PM (read in-browser): "We identified a bot farm of approximately 200,000 accounts. Within this farm, we found 200 accounts posting in a manner that could manipulate a legitimate debate about American AI and energy policy. These posts contained claims that AI data centers are driving up household electricity prices and straining the grid. Others included AI-generated cartoons that depicted data-center operators enriching themselves at the public's expense." No methodology, no dates, no engagement figures, no dataset. The announcement itself: 3.2M views. https://x.com/GlobalAffairs/status/2093130747796148634
  • ★ OpenAI, 10 Jun 2026, "PRC-linked influence operations are targeting AI debates in the US" (read in-browser): "The targeting of OpenAI and US data center buildouts is significant not because the operation appears to have shifted public opinion, but because it shows PRC-origin influence operators testing narratives against AI infrastructure … The operation sought to exploit and amplify existing public concerns about energy prices and local impacts of data center development, but we found no evidence of meaningful breakout beyond its own activity." https://openai.com/index/prc-linked-influence-operations-ai-debates/
  • ★ The "Data Center Bandwagon" case study, 1 Jun 2026 (read in-browser): a cluster "that likely originated in China"; operators "likely part of a social media operations team at a private Chinese technology company conducting work for Chinese provincial-level government clients"; content posted on X with #capacityauction, comics about a grid operator's capacity-auction prices "based on reporting from a legitimate regional paper"; VPNs to reach ChatGPT; a work report analysing Facebook's groups, ads and recommendation systems for expansion. No Breakout Scale rating on this page; ★ the sister cluster "Tech and Tariffs" is rated "Category One … no evidence of breakout. Most of the social media posts we identified generated little or no observable engagement." https://openai.com/index/disrupting-malicious-uses-of-ai-data-center-bandwagon/ · https://openai.com/index/disrupting-malicious-uses-of-ai-tech-and-tariffs/
  • ★ The organic baseline (ep17's verified record): NBC/SurveyMonkey 20 Aug–1 Sep, 69% oppose a data center locally (R 57 / I 71 / D 81); Gallup, fielded 2–18 Mar, published 13 May, 70–71%; Quinnipiac PA 68% (Feb) → 74% (Jul).

Settled facts (not the debate)

  1. ★ A PRC-linked operation on the US data-center debate existed. OpenAI documented it (1 and 10 Jun); X suspended the accounts; nobody disputes the accounts were inauthentic. Attribution is "likely" a private Chinese contractor working for provincial-government clients — not a named state organ.
  2. ★/○ It was small and short, and its discoverers say so. OpenAI: "no evidence of meaningful breakout beyond its own activity"; the sister cluster rated Category One. ○ Linvill (Clemson), who reviewed the suspended accounts: created late 2025, posted Dec 2025–Jan 2026, two or three posts each, "a few hundred posts", at least 59 with zero engagement, "no real humans saw these posts about data centers"; nothing since January. ○ Nimmo (OpenAI, NPR 10 Jun): "we didn't see signs that they succeeded." ⚠ These are null findings, not proof of no effect.
  3. ★ X's August disclosure and OpenAI's June report describe the SAME operation. X's post carries no methodology, dates or engagement; ○ X's own account (Engadget) and Linvill both identify the ~200 as OpenAI's cluster re-reported. There are not two independent findings. And 200 of 200,000 is 0.1% — the headline number is the whole suspected network, not the data-center content.
  4. ★ The "foreign money" is European climate philanthropy, and no grant names data centers. AEI (Apr 2026, 5 pp): "$39M+ from foreign donors to 12 organizations" — the donors are Wyss (CH), Hohn (UK), Quadrature (UK), KR (DK), Oak (UK); six organisations are named in the text; no per-grant figures or purposes. APT (Oct 2025, 31 pp, read by pypdf): "almost $2 billion" from Quadrature, KR, Oak, Laudes and CIFF, a multi-year stock back to 2008–2015; the words "data center" do not appear; the one 350.org grant purpose found is "fossil fuel divestment campaigns" (KR, >$300k); Quadrature to 350.org $950k (2020–present). ○ Both authors are funded chiefly through DonorsTrust, a donor-advised fund that does not disclose its donors.
  5. ○ The one China-MONEY thread on the record is Singham's network (Bitcoin Policy Institute, 18 May 2026: Neville Roy Singham, Shanghai-based; Tricontinental, People's Forum, CodePink; a 2023 NYT investigation, a 2024 Delhi Police chargesheet) — and it is not tied to any data-center campaign in the record. BPI's report is one of the two documents the June House letter rests on.
  6. ★ Congress has asked; it has not found. The Energy & Commerce letter (Guthrie, Joyce, Latta, 4 Jun 2026, 3 pp, read by pypdf) to PCAST co-chairs Sacks and Kratsios and FBI Director Patel "requests a briefing no later than June 18, 2026"; it cites the Bitcoin Policy Institute and Power the Future, contains no FARA reference, and its "$45.8 billion in data center projects disrupted" in Virginia is Power the Future's figure (whose report's own thesis is "an anti-Trump movement wearing a hard hat"). ○ The FBI's on-record reply: "In accordance with Department of Justice policy, the FBI can neither confirm nor deny conducting specific investigations." No briefing, hearing, DOJ FARA action or NGO response is on the record as of 14 Sep.
  7. ○ The federal referee is gone. The FBI's Foreign Influence Task Force was disbanded in Feb 2025; ODNI's Foreign Malign Influence Center was curtailed in Aug 2025 (statute bars formal closure before 31 Dec 2028); Warner/Padilla (10 Jun 2026) describe the counter-influence apparatus in retreat. The grading is being done by a platform, an AI lab, a university lab and advocacy shops.
  8. ★ The professional yardstick is the Breakout Scale (Nimmo, Brookings): Category One = one community, one platform; Six = a policy response or concrete action. It grades how far content TRAVELLED, not how many accounts existed. By it, the operation never left its own accounts — and the amplification of its discovery (a cabinet secretary, two House committees, a president) is the part of this story that reached Categories Five and Six.
  9. ○ No independent threat-intel shop corroborates a China network on US energy siting. Meta's H2 2026 report: no such network; Anthropic's Sep 2026 report: none; ★ Mandiant's DRAGONBRIDGE precedent: a PRC-attributed actor tried to physically mobilise Americans against Lynas's Texas rare-earths plant in 2021 and 2022 and "was unsuccessful" both times — intent on the record, capability not.
  10. ★/○ The opposition is a majority in every party, every region and every poll since February, and predates the posts. NBC 69% (R 57 / I 71 / D 81); Gallup 70–71%; Quinnipiac 68→74. ○ >99% of $31M in 2026 political ads mentioning data centers run against them (domestic money). Peculiar MO blocked a campus in Oct 2024; St Charles MO's NDA fight was public by Aug 2025; the 230-group moratorium letter is Dec 2025 — all before or at the posting window. ○ Cheyenne, May 2026: the petition organiser called the foreign-influence narrative "ridiculous … this community is full of professionals and experts."
  11. ○ Sequence. Opposition polling: Feb (Quinnipiac 68%), Mar (Gallup fielded). First "foreign dark money" claim: Cheyenne LEADS' CEO, c. late Apr; Interior Sec. Burgum, 11 May ("It's not organic and local") — two days before Gallup PUBLISHED, two months after it was fielded. OpenAI: 1/10 Jun. Abbott 3 Aug, Shapiro 18 Aug (no China), Ayotte 2 Sep. X: 27 Aug. The China-specific attributions (31 Aug; 10–11 Sep) trail every poll and every Republican reversal. ⚠ "The accusation arrived the week the polling turned" is WRONG and stays out of the script.
  12. ○ Data-center backlashes elsewhere had no foreign hand on the record: Ireland (grid constraints), Singapore (power/water/land), the Netherlands (land use), Chile (aquifer). They show grid-and-water backlashes happen without one; they do not show this one had none.

The contested core (both steelmanned)

GREEN — a foreign hand on American anger. The operation is real, PRC-origin by the discoverer's own attribution, and precisely aimed: household bills, grid strain, PJM's capacity auction — the exact talking points that now dominate. It planned a Facebook expansion in writing. The same actor class tried to mobilise Americans against a Texas industrial site twice (Mandiant), which puts intent on the record. Nobody official is counting: the task force and the center that would grade this are gone, and a platform's "we found 200" with no methodology can under-report as easily as over-report. European foreign principals fund the coalition asking Congress for a national moratorium, which is exactly what FARA exists to surface. And "no evidence of breakout" is an unsearched negative: nobody looked at Facebook or a single local group.

GOLD — a non-story dressed as a national threat. One operation, twice disclosed. A few hundred posts, most with zero engagement, eight months old, suspended, rated at the bottom of the professional scale by the people who found it. The anger has documented domestic causes on the record — a four-minute hearing, dry wells, NDAs, a regional bill the market monitor attributes 46% to data centers — and a majority in every party. The "$39M foreign" is Swiss, British and Danish philanthropy that never mentions data centers, reported by shops funded through a fund that discloses nothing. The accusers removed the referee and now assert the count. The China-specific claims arrived after the polls and after three Republican governors had already moved.

What each side must concede. GREEN: there is one primary, not two; X published nothing checkable; nothing in the record shows the operation reached anyone. GOLD: "no breakout" is a null finding by parties grading their own work; the Facebook plan and the DRAGONBRIDGE precedent put intent on the record; and the absence of a referee cuts both ways.

The reframe — what the shouting gets wrong

"Did China try?" and "did China cause it?" are different questions, and the record answers them differently: a PRC-linked contractor demonstrably tried; nothing shows it caused anything. The useful question for a county board is not "is this a psyop?" but "what would it take to tell?" — and the professionals have an answer, the Breakout Scale. By it, the operation never left its own accounts; the only part of this story that reached the top of the scale is the domestic amplification of its discovery. Second: the "foreign money" strand and the "Chinese bots" strand are two different accusations welded together on air. One is European climate philanthropy funding US NGOs (legal, partly disclosed, unconnected to data centers in any grant found); the other is a suspended X network. Neither supports the other.

Both quietly agree

Nobody with the tools to measure this is measuring it. Both sides depend on numbers produced by interested parties — a platform grading its own takedown, an AI lab grading its own tool's misuse, advocacy shops grading their opponents' donors, committees citing those shops — while the FBI task force, the ODNI center and any independent forensics review are absent. Both would rather have a referee.

Verdict framing (no winner — hand it to the viewer)

The record supports "it happened AND it was tiny." What is contested is what a real-but-tiny operation licenses: a document demand on the Sierra Club, a presidential explanation for a 69% majority, or nothing. The dial: how much reach a foreign operation must show before its existence is allowed to explain your neighbours — one post, one platform, one real reader, one changed vote?

Script traps

  • "Two independent primaries confirm" — one operation, twice disclosed.
  • "200,000 Chinese bots pushed anti-data-center content" — 200 did; 200,000 is the suspected network.
  • "OpenAI rated the data-center operation Category One" — only the sister cluster carries the label; say "no evidence of meaningful breakout, and the sister cluster rated at the lowest tier."
  • "It's proven to have had no effect" — null findings by the discoverers, not proof.
  • "$39 million in foreign money" next to "China" — say Swiss, British, Danish.
  • "The FBI is investigating" — it can neither confirm nor deny; Congress requested a briefing.
  • "The accusation arrived the week the polling turned" — it trailed the polls by months.
  • "Republicans support, Democrats oppose" — 57% of Republicans oppose.
  • Any attribution of MOTIVE to the President, the committees, OpenAI or X.
  • O'Leary's named individuals; any Wisconsin county.

Open questions carried to the script

  • Did the Facebook expansion ever execute? Has any PRC-linked content reached a local group, petition or hearing anywhere?
  • What do the five 10 Sep letters demand, with what deadline, in their own text?
  • Did the 18 Jun PCAST/FBI briefing happen?
  • Has Chinese state media cheered the backlash — independent evidence of interest distinct from covert accounts?
  • Is there ANY case of a foreign operation measurably moving a US zoning decision?
