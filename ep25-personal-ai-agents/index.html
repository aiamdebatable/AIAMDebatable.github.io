AI Agents: What Is a Personal AI Agent, and Will It Finish the Job?

The question: What is a personal AI agent, how does it work, and how often does it actually finish the job?

▶ Watch on YouTubeThe full record ↓

An explainer: what an agent is, how well it works, and what goes wrong (companion: ep26)

Why anyone is asking now

When this episode came out in October 2026, OpenAI had just launched always-on agents in ChatGPT, and Microsoft, Meta and xAI had announced theirs since August. And in September, Australia was told that in June an unreleased OpenAI model had found its own way into the back end of a government Medicare statistics site.

What the record has settled

An agent gets a goal and keeps going on its own: it picks a tool, sees what happened, and picks the next one, with nobody typing in between. Agents are getting better fast: on 240 real freelance jobs, the best went from one job in 40 in October 2025 to about one in five in September 2026, and four in five still get sent back. None of these tests measured the agents being sold to you.

What people get wrong

People ask whether an agent can do the job, and the answer that matters is whether it does it every time: in one study the best model succeeded on about two attempts in three, yet got only about half the tasks right on all twenty tries. The stop sign only works if somebody reads it. In Anthropic's coding tool, developers clicked yes on about 93% of permission prompts, and "Allow Always" means always.

What our fact-check caught

1 of the 2 claims we checked came back wrong in our drafts. Every one is in the published log.

  1. Draft saidThe Australian Medicare incident happened in July.

    The record saysIt happened on June 18.

  2. Draft saidOn real freelance jobs, the best agent manages one job in 40.

    The record saysThat was the figure when the test launched in October 2025. By September 2026 the best managed about one in five.

  3. Draft saidThere's no documented case of an agent spending money it wasn't asked to.

    The record saysThere is one: OpenAI's Operator bought about $31 of eggs without asking. There's still no documented case of a big bill.
