H.R. 9925 FRONTIER Act (govinfo PDF, 74 pp, pypdf)✅ Introduced 07-23 (six sponsors). "Frontier model" = >10^26 operations; "very large frontier developer" = >$5 B revenue AND ≥$10 B AI development spend over 36 months, with affiliates. Sec. 5: very large developers must retain an IVO licensed by the Under Secretary of Commerce for AI Security; "timely access upon request to unredacted materials, records, personnel, systems"; reports at least every 6 months, simultaneously to the Under Secretary and the developer; the lead assessor certifies the IVO "is free from conflicts of interest". Sec. 3(c): licensing rules must include "conflict-of-interest and funding-transparency requirements" and a finding that the applicant "has demonstrated its independence from the artificial intelligence industry". Civil penalty "not more than $1,000,000 per violation", each day a separate violation. Sec. 7(b): state preemption of new substantive obligations on developers. Sec. 8: the Secretary may issue an emergency order "suspending or restricting" development, deployment or internal use on a finding of imminent catastrophic risk. Sec. 5(j) "Public disclosure of assessment reports": within 30 days of receiving an IVO report the developer must "conspicuously publish on a publicly available website … a high-level summary of the findings of such report and a copy" with redactions limited to trade secrets / risk-prevention mechanisms / cybersecurity / public safety / national security / legal compliance, each redaction with a written justification. So the public DOES see the report, redacted by the company under rules — the same shape as Amodei's offer, with a licence and a penalty behind it.

⛔ CORRECTION 2026-09-27, after publication: two Anthropic facts were misstated on air

Found during later research and re-read at both primaries before publication. The fix came too late for the cut, so the published video carries both errors and they are corrected here, in the transcript note and in the video description.

  • "Anthropic could reach two" of the 15 hosts. Wrong unit. The July 30 post: "We notified … the three affected organizations on Monday, July 27. The two organizations we were able to reach had not previously detected the activity." That is two of three organisations. The 15 hosts were, in Anthropic's belief, security vendors whose scanners installed the package (alignment assessment). The brief's line "could reach two of the 15 affected hosts" carried the error into the script.
  • "Widened the search … and found a fourth incident." Wrong order. The alignment assessment says the fourth was found in August in transcripts missed by the first scan, "while assembling transcripts to share with METR". "After finding this incident, we broadened our search to roughly 481 million transcripts", and that sweep "found no further cases". The 141,006 figure on screen is correct.
