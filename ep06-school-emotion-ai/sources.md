## Who's affected / the stakes
- Human Rights Watch (2022): 164 ed-tech products, 49 countries; 145 of 163 (~89%) surveilled or could
  surveil children — https://www.hrw.org/news/2022/07/12/online-learning-products-enabled-surveillance-children
- Racial bias: Face++ scored Black faces ~2× as angry; Face API ~3× more "contemptuous" (400 NBA
  players; Lauren Rhue) — https://theconversation.com/emotion-reading-tech-fails-the-racial-bias-test-108404

## The science
- Barrett, Adolphs, Marsella, Martinez & Pollak (2019), "Emotional Expressions Reconsidered,"
  *Psychological Science in the Public Interest* — 1,000+ studies; facial movements not "reliable and
  specific" enough to diagnose emotion — https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6640856/
- UK ICO (Oct 2022): emotion analysis "immature," "may not work yet, or indeed ever"; first-of-kind
  warning (Deputy Commissioner Stephen Bonner) — https://techcrunch.com/2022/10/26/no-to-voight-kampff-tests/
- ARTICLE 19 "Emotional Entanglement" (2021) and AI Now (2019) called for limits/bans (context) —
  https://www.article19.org/emotion-recognition-technology-report/

## The two loaded concepts / the EU line
- Emotion-recognition definition + scope; "engagement" rebranding; accuracy vs construct validity —
  Future of Privacy Forum:
  https://fpf.org/blog/red-lines-under-eu-ai-act-unpacking-the-prohibition-of-emotion-recognition-in-the-workplace-and-education-institutions/
- EU AI Act Article 5(1)(f): prohibits emotion inference in education; in force 2 Feb 2025;
  medical/safety exception; Recital 18 excludes pain/fatigue + plain frowns/smiles —
  https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/article/5/
- Max penalty €35M or 7% of global turnover — Wolters Kluwer:
  https://legalblogs.wolterskluwer.com/global-workplace-law-and-policy/the-prohibition-of-ai-emotion-recognition-technologies-in-the-workplace-under-the-ai-act/
