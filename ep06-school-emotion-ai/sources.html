Sources — "AI emotion-recognition in schools: ban it, or bound it?"

Grouped by claim. Every on-screen number and named source traces here. Verdicts in factcheck-log.md.

What it is / where deployed

  • Intel + Classroom Technologies ("Class"/Zoom) engagement-detection AI (2022) — AI Incident Database: https://incidentdatabase.ai/reports/2301/ ; campaign context (Techdirt): https://www.techdirt.com/2022/04/20/intel-wants-to-add-unproven-emotion-detection-ai-to-distance-learning-tech/
  • Exam proctoring flags gaze/movement/whispering; harms disabled/neurodivergent students — EFF: https://www.eff.org/deeplinks/2021/06/long-overdue-reckoning-online-proctoring-companies-may-finally-be-here ; CDT: https://cdt.org/insights/how-automated-test-proctoring-software-discriminates-against-disabled-students/
  • Hangzhou No. 11 Middle School: cameras scanned faces ~every 30 sec into ~7 emotion categories; students self-policed expression — Globe & Mail: https://www.theglobeandmail.com/world/article-in-china-classroom-cameras-scan-student-faces-for-emotion-stoking/
  • Emotion-AI market ~$2.74B (2024) → ~$9.01B (2030) — MarketsandMarkets: https://www.marketsandmarkets.com/Market-Reports/emotion-ai-market-134111673.html

Who's affected / the stakes

  • Human Rights Watch (2022): 164 ed-tech products, 49 countries; 145 of 163 (~89%) surveilled or could surveil children — https://www.hrw.org/news/2022/07/12/online-learning-products-enabled-surveillance-children
  • Racial bias: Face++ scored Black faces ~2× as angry; Face API ~3× more "contemptuous" (400 NBA players; Lauren Rhue) — https://theconversation.com/emotion-reading-tech-fails-the-racial-bias-test-108404

The science

  • Barrett, Adolphs, Marsella, Martinez & Pollak (2019), "Emotional Expressions Reconsidered," Psychological Science in the Public Interest — 1,000+ studies; facial movements not "reliable and specific" enough to diagnose emotion — https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6640856/
  • UK ICO (Oct 2022): emotion analysis "immature," "may not work yet, or indeed ever"; first-of-kind warning (Deputy Commissioner Stephen Bonner) — https://techcrunch.com/2022/10/26/no-to-voight-kampff-tests/
  • ARTICLE 19 "Emotional Entanglement" (2021) and AI Now (2019) called for limits/bans (context) — https://www.article19.org/emotion-recognition-technology-report/

The two loaded concepts / the EU line

  • Emotion-recognition definition + scope; "engagement" rebranding; accuracy vs construct validity — Future of Privacy Forum: https://fpf.org/blog/red-lines-under-eu-ai-act-unpacking-the-prohibition-of-emotion-recognition-in-the-workplace-and-education-institutions/
  • EU AI Act Article 5(1)(f): prohibits emotion inference in education; in force 2 Feb 2025; medical/safety exception; Recital 18 excludes pain/fatigue + plain frowns/smiles — https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/article/5/
  • Max penalty €35M or 7% of global turnover — Wolters Kluwer: https://legalblogs.wolterskluwer.com/global-workplace-law-and-policy/the-prohibition-of-ai-emotion-recognition-technologies-in-the-workplace-under-the-ai-act/

Accessibility (the steelman for bounded use)

  • Emotion-recognition research built with/for autistic learners — Springer (Soft Computing): https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00500-017-2549-z
  • "Towards affective computing that works for everyone" (inclusive design vs prohibition) — https://scholarlypublications.universiteitleiden.nl/access/item:3719677/download
