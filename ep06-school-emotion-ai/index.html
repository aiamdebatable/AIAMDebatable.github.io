The claims
  • Confirmed

    Emotion AI claims to infer inner states from face/voice/posture; deployed as engagement monitoring, exam proctoring, classroom cameras

  • Confirmed

    One Chinese school (Hangzhou No. 11) scanned faces ~every 30 seconds into ~7 emotion categories

  • Confirmed

    Emotion-AI market ~$2.7B (2024), projected to more than triple (~$9B) by 2030

  • Confirmed

    Human Rights Watch (2022): reviewed 164 ed-tech products; ~89% (145 of 163) could surveil children, often with no opt-out

  • Confirmed

    Commercial tools scored Black faces as angrier / more "contemptuous" than white faces, even when smiling (400 NBA players)

  • Confirmed

    Barrett et al. (2019), Psychological Science in the Public Interest: reviewed 1,000+ studies; concluded you can't reliably infer emotion from facial expression

  • Confirmed

    Emotion classifiers can be "accurate" at a facial configuration while lacking construct validity (measuring the face ≠ the feeling)

  • Confirmed

    UK ICO (2022) called emotion analysis "immature," warned it "may not work yet, or indeed ever"

  • Confirmed

    EU AI Act Article 5(1)(f) prohibits AI emotion inference in education; in force 2 Feb 2025; medical/safety exception kept; plain expressions + pain/fatigue excluded

  • Confirmed

    Max EU penalty €35M or 7% of global turnover

  • Confirmed

    Accessibility research: emotion-recognition tools built with/for autistic learners exist

  • Confirmed

    Students self-policed expression under classroom face-scanning (Hangzhou)

  • Approximate

    Barrett meta-analytic effect sizes (r≈.32; ~15–25%)
