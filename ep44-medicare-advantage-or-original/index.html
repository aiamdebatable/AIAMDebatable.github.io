The verdict

Where the record leans
Where the record leans: for a healthy person who'd otherwise have no supplement, the bundle is the better deal. For someone who can afford one and wants any doctor and a way back, no clear saving from switching, and real costs in a sick year. And the public pays about fourteen percent more per enrollee.
What didn't hold up
Advantage always saves you money: it depends on the comparison. Original Medicare never says no: it does. Half the doctors in your directory are fake: that was mental health, in ten counties. People in Advantage die sooner: the record doesn't show it. And almost every appeal wins: only among the few who appealed.

Read the verdict as it aired

Mara This one doesn't resolve into a yes or a no, and neither team wins it.

Mara What survived both. Any protection beats none: against no extra coverage, a plan cuts costs, and so does a supplement. Against Original Medicare as most people hold it, no clear saving either way. Plans ask permission far more often, most sharply after a hospital stay, and in most states the door back is narrow.

Mara What fell. Advantage always saves you money: it depends on the comparison. Original Medicare never says no: it does. Half the doctors in your directory are fake: that was mental health, in ten counties. People in Advantage die sooner: the record doesn't show it. And almost every appeal wins: only among the few who appealed.

Mara Where the record leans: for a healthy person who'd otherwise have no supplement, the bundle is the better deal. For someone who can afford one and wants any doctor and a way back, no clear saving from switching, and real costs in a sick year. And the public pays about fourteen percent more per enrollee.

Mara So the dial has four settings, and none is free. The open door: Original Medicare, a supplement around two hundred dollars a month, and a drug plan, with any doctor who takes Medicare. Its weak point: a premium that keeps rising, and outside a window, one priced on your health, or refused.

Mara The bundle: an Advantage plan, often no extra premium and a cap; its weak point is permission, a network, and a door that may close. Bare: Original Medicare alone, no yearly limit. And both programs: Medicaid or a Savings Program pays the costs, if you sign up. A lower bill, or an open door later?

Mara Whichever you choose, check these this month; open enrollment ends December seventh. First, your network: the Medicare Plan Finder shows if a plan has your doctors, but call the office. Second, your drugs, in the Plan Finder. Third, your supplement window: at sixty-five, a trial year, March fourth if your plan ends, or state rules.

Mara Fourth, whether you qualify for Savings Programs or Extra Help. Last, free, unbiased help: State Health Insurance Assistance Programs at ship help dot org, or one eight hundred Medicare. Brokers are paid by the plans they sell, up to just over seven hundred dollars a sign-up. Which year are you buying for? That one's yours.

The judge named no sides on this one, so there's no dial: we don't invent positions.
