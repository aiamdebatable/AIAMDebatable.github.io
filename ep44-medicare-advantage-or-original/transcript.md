## The research · Who pays for the extras
**Ryan:** Medicare pays each plan a set amount per member. MedPAC, Congress's Medicare advisers, estimates it will spend about fourteen percent more for people in Advantage than the same people would cost in Original Medicare. That's roughly seventy-six billion dollars this year.
