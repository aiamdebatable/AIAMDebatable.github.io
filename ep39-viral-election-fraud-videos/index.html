Viral Election-Fraud Videos: What Did They Really Show?

The question: Viral election-fraud videos: what did they really show, and how can you tell?

▶ Watch on YouTubeThe full record ↓

Why anyone is asking now

When this episode came out in late October 2026, just before the November vote, the FBI held Fulton County, Georgia's 2020 ballots, seized that January. Posts were also claiming the Post Office wouldn't deliver ballots to states that refused to hand over their voter rolls.

What the record has settled

Almost every viral clip the episode opened, from every side, was explained: ordinary steps that look sinister without the rulebook, or footage that was staged. Real fraud does happen, but it was small and caught by records, and in the record checked, six races were thrown out or run again, none statewide. Fulton's double scans and repeated images are proven errors, and whether any of it was intentional is still open.

What people get wrong

The famous State Farm clip showed real ballots, already opened, stored under a table, and Georgia's hand count of about five million paper ballots matched the machines to within about a tenth of a percent. The better questions to ask of a clip are what should be on screen if it were normal, and who has already checked. The record leans hard but not all the way: fraud nobody caught can't be counted, so nobody should claim a number for it, either way.
