The claims
  • Confirmed

    Bartz v. Anthropic decided June 2025, Judge William Alsup (N.D. Cal.)

  • Confirmed

    Alsup called LLM training "exceedingly/spectacularly transformative"

  • Confirmed

    Anthropic downloaded 7M+ pirated books (LibGen, PiLiMi); keeping them ruled infringement, to trial

  • Confirmed

    Digitizing lawfully-purchased print books held fair use

  • Confirmed

    Bartz piracy settlement = $1.5 billion; ~500,000 titles; ~$3,000/work

  • Confirmed

    Kadrey v. Meta (June 2025, Judge Chhabria); 13 authors; "highly transformative" but ruling doesn't make it "lawful"; plaintiffs "made the wrong arguments"

  • Confirmed

    Chhabria: "market dilution will often cause plaintiffs to decisively win the fourth factor"

  • Confirmed

    Thomson Reuters v. Ross: NOT fair use; AI trained on Westlaw to build a rival; factor four (market) decisive; on appeal to 3rd Cir.

  • Confirmed

    US Copyright Office Part 3 (May 2025): AI training "not categorically fair use"; competing-audience use "at best modestly transformative"; rejects "inherently transformative"; market dilution cognizable

  • Confirmed

    70+ copyright lawsuits vs AI companies (trackers 50–70+)

  • Confirmed

    April 2025: 12 OpenAI/Microsoft cases consolidated into one SDNY MDL

  • Confirmed

    13 novelists sued Meta; NYT sued OpenAI (survived motion to dismiss Mar 2025)

  • Confirmed

    Common Crawl ~9.5 PB, 300B+ pages; 80%+ of GPT-3's tokens; ~45% ToS-restricted (2024 study)

  • Confirmed

    LLMs train on ~hundreds of billions up to ~20 trillion tokens

  • Confirmed

    "Books3" ~196,000 pirated books; named in Meta's LLaMA paper

  • Confirmed

    News Corp–OpenAI reported >$250M/5yr; Reddit–Google ~$60M/yr

  • Confirmed

    UK: Getty v. Stability (Nov 2025) — "does not store the training data itself"; Getty lost secondary copyright claim

  • Confirmed

    The four fair-use factors (17 U.S.C. §107)

  • Unresolved

    Kadrey exact factor-by-factor tally
