# Sources — "Training AI on copyrighted work: fair use, or theft?"

_Grouped by claim. Every on-screen number and named ruling traces here. Verdicts in `factcheck-log.md`._

## Scale of training data
- Common Crawl ~9.5 PB, 300B+ pages, grows 3–5B/month; 80%+ of GPT-3 tokens; ~200B–20T tokens per
  model; ~45% ToS-restricted (2024 study) — Mozilla Foundation:
  https://www.mozillafoundation.org/en/research/library/generative-ai-training-data/common-crawl/ ;
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Common_Crawl
- "Books3" ~196,000 pirated books (from Bibliotik), in Meta's LLaMA paper — Authors Guild:
  https://authorsguild.org/news/meta-libgen-ai-training-book-heist-what-authors-need-to-know/ ;
  Gizmodo: https://gizmodo.com/anti-piracy-group-takes-ai-training-dataset-books3-off-1850743763

## Bartz v. Anthropic (the settled line)
- June 2025, Judge Alsup; training "exceedingly transformative"; pirated acquisition = separate wrong
  to trial; digitizing purchased print books = fair use — Wiggin & Dana:
  https://www.wiggin.com/publication/bartz-v-anthropic-first-court-decision-on-fair-use-defense-in-llm-training/ ;
  Akin Gump: https://www.akingump.com/en/insights/ai-law-and-regulation-tracker/district-court-rules-ai-training-can-be-fair-use-in-bartz-v-anthropic
- $1.5B settlement; ~500,000 titles; ~$3,000/work; holding on purchased books applied only to named
  plaintiffs — Authors Guild:
  https://authorsguild.org/advocacy/artificial-intelligence/what-authors-need-to-know-about-the-anthropic-settlement/

## Kadrey v. Meta
- June 2025, Judge Chhabria; 13 authors; "highly transformative" but ruling "does not stand for the
  proposition that Meta's use... is lawful"; plaintiffs "made the wrong arguments"; "market dilution
  will often cause plaintiffs to decisively win the fourth factor" — Goodwin (above).

## Thomson Reuters v. Ross
- Feb 2025, Judge Bibas; NOT fair use; AI trained on Westlaw headnotes to build a rival; factor four
  decisive; on appeal to 3rd Circuit — Reed Smith:
  https://www.reedsmith.com/articles/court-ai-fair-use-thomson-reuters-enterprise-gmbh-ross-intelligence/ ;
  Jenner & Block: https://www.jenner.com/en/news-insights/client-alerts/court-decides-that-use-of-copyrighted-works-in-ai-training-is-not-fair-use-thomson-reuters-enterprise-centre-gmbh-v-ross-intelligence-inc ;
  IPWatchdog (appeal): https://ipwatchdog.com/2025/10/01/amici-back-ai-companys-third-circuit-appeal-summary-judgment-thomson-reuters/

## US Copyright Office — Part 3 (Generative AI Training)
- Released May 2025; "not categorically fair use"; competing-audience use "at best modestly
  transformative"; rejects "inherently transformative"; market dilution cognizable — Crowell & Moring:
  https://www.crowell.com/en/insights/client-alerts/us-copyright-office-releases-third-report-on-ai-and-copyright-addressing-training-ai-models-with-copyrighted-materials ;
  Copyright Alliance: https://copyrightalliance.org/copyright-offices-ai-report-takeaways/
