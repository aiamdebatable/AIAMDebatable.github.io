Sources — "Training AI on copyrighted work: fair use, or theft?"

Grouped by claim. Every on-screen number and named ruling traces here. Verdicts in factcheck-log.md.

Scale of training data

  • Common Crawl ~9.5 PB, 300B+ pages, grows 3–5B/month; 80%+ of GPT-3 tokens; ~200B–20T tokens per model; ~45% ToS-restricted (2024 study) — Mozilla Foundation: https://www.mozillafoundation.org/en/research/library/generative-ai-training-data/common-crawl/ ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Common_Crawl
  • "Books3" ~196,000 pirated books (from Bibliotik), in Meta's LLaMA paper — Authors Guild: https://authorsguild.org/news/meta-libgen-ai-training-book-heist-what-authors-need-to-know/ ; Gizmodo: https://gizmodo.com/anti-piracy-group-takes-ai-training-dataset-books3-off-1850743763

The litigation landscape

  • 70+ lawsuits (trackers 50–70+) — Copyright Alliance: https://copyrightalliance.org/ai-copyright-lawsuit-developments-2025/ ; case tracker: https://chatgptiseatingtheworld.com/2025/10/08/status-of-all-51-copyright-lawsuits-v-ai-oct-8-2025-no-more-decisions-on-fair-use-in-2025/
  • 13 authors v. Meta; NYT v. OpenAI (survived MTD Mar 26 2025) — Goodwin: https://www.goodwinlaw.com/en/insights/publications/2025/06/alerts-practices-aiml-northern-district-of-california-judge-rules ; NPR: https://www.npr.org/2025/03/26/nx-s1-5288157/new-york-times-openai-copyright-case-goes-forward
  • April 2025 MDL consolidated 12 OpenAI/Microsoft cases in SDNY — Copyright Alliance (above).

Bartz v. Anthropic (the settled line)

  • June 2025, Judge Alsup; training "exceedingly transformative"; pirated acquisition = separate wrong to trial; digitizing purchased print books = fair use — Wiggin & Dana: https://www.wiggin.com/publication/bartz-v-anthropic-first-court-decision-on-fair-use-defense-in-llm-training/ ; Akin Gump: https://www.akingump.com/en/insights/ai-law-and-regulation-tracker/district-court-rules-ai-training-can-be-fair-use-in-bartz-v-anthropic
  • $1.5B settlement; ~500,000 titles; ~$3,000/work; holding on purchased books applied only to named plaintiffs — Authors Guild: https://authorsguild.org/advocacy/artificial-intelligence/what-authors-need-to-know-about-the-anthropic-settlement/

Kadrey v. Meta

  • June 2025, Judge Chhabria; 13 authors; "highly transformative" but ruling "does not stand for the proposition that Meta's use... is lawful"; plaintiffs "made the wrong arguments"; "market dilution will often cause plaintiffs to decisively win the fourth factor" — Goodwin (above).

Thomson Reuters v. Ross

  • Feb 2025, Judge Bibas; NOT fair use; AI trained on Westlaw headnotes to build a rival; factor four decisive; on appeal to 3rd Circuit — Reed Smith: https://www.reedsmith.com/articles/court-ai-fair-use-thomson-reuters-enterprise-gmbh-ross-intelligence/ ; Jenner & Block: https://www.jenner.com/en/news-insights/client-alerts/court-decides-that-use-of-copyrighted-works-in-ai-training-is-not-fair-use-thomson-reuters-enterprise-centre-gmbh-v-ross-intelligence-inc ; IPWatchdog (appeal): https://ipwatchdog.com/2025/10/01/amici-back-ai-companys-third-circuit-appeal-summary-judgment-thomson-reuters/

US Copyright Office — Part 3 (Generative AI Training)

  • Released May 2025; "not categorically fair use"; competing-audience use "at best modestly transformative"; rejects "inherently transformative"; market dilution cognizable — Crowell & Moring: https://www.crowell.com/en/insights/client-alerts/us-copyright-office-releases-third-report-on-ai-and-copyright-addressing-training-ai-models-with-copyrighted-materials ; Copyright Alliance: https://copyrightalliance.org/copyright-offices-ai-report-takeaways/

UK — Getty v. Stability

  • Nov 2025 ([2025] EWHC 2863 (Ch)); "does not store the training data itself"; Getty lost secondary copyright claim — Ropes & Gray: https://www.ropesgray.com/en/insights/viewpoints/102lvxe/getty-image-loses-copyright-infringement-claim-against-stability-ai-in-uks-first

The licensing market (reported figures)

  • News Corp–OpenAI >$250M/5yr; Reddit–Google ~$60M/yr; Axel Springer ~$13M/yr; FT ~$5–10M/yr — Similarweb: https://www.similarweb.com/blog/daas/data-analysis/data-licensing-deals/ ; Digiday: https://digiday.com/media/2024-in-review-a-timeline-of-the-major-deals-between-publishers-and-ai-companies/

Statute
